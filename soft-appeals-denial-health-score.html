The weighting is published because a score you cannot check is a score you cannot use.

Each question is worth 0 to 4 points, multiplied by the weight below. Deadline tracking and submission evidence carry the heaviest weight because a missed window or a receipt dispute can close a claim outright, whichever way the underlying argument would have gone. Reporting carries less because thin reporting costs visibility rather than claims.
