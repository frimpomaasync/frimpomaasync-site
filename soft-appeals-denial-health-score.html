Soft Appeals · Denial Health Score Process diagnostic · no patient information

What this is. A process diagnostic covering how denied claims are owned, tracked, evidenced, routed, prioritized, followed up, reconciled and reported. The weighting behind the score is published below, so the result can be checked rather than taken on trust.

What this is not. It does not assess regulatory or legal compliance, does not evaluate your privacy or security program, does not review coding, and makes no statement about any specific claim or the reimbursement any claim might produce. It is an operational read of a workflow, based only on what you selected.

The twelve areas, and what each is worth

The weighting is published because a score you cannot check is a score you cannot use.

Each question is worth 0 to 4 points, multiplied by the weight below. Deadline tracking and submission evidence carry the heaviest weight because a missed window or a receipt dispute can close a claim outright, whichever way the underlying argument would have gone. Reporting carries less because thin reporting costs visibility rather than claims.
