This is not a list of everything. It is the short list of things that removed a real problem from a real week, plus the free fix to try first in every case.

The desk. Six problems, six fixes.

Every one of these started as an annoyance that cost ten minutes a day. Fix the annoyance and the desk stops being a place you avoid.

Paper with nowhere to go

One labelled drawer or tray.

Not filing. Just one named place so paper stops living in a pile that you move rather than clear. Try a shoebox first. If the box works, buy the tray.

Phone flat before a job

A charging spot that is always the same spot.

One cable, one place, plugged in before you sit down. The free version is deciding where it lives and never moving it.

Today's post lost by Thursday

An in tray for what arrived and is not dealt with.

It empties on a Friday. That is the entire method.

Cannot see the week

A printed week on the wall.

Free, and it beats a calendar app for the one thing that matters: seeing the overbooked day before you agree to it.

Cables everywhere

Tie them once.

Ten minutes, a handful of ties, and it never needs doing again. The cheapest hour of your year.

Nowhere to work

One clear surface for the job in progress.

Not the whole desk. One surface that is allowed to be empty. Protect it.

If you make content

Three things, in this order.

Camera quality is almost never the problem. Steadiness, light and sound are, and in that order. Your phone is already good enough for all three.

1 · Something to hold the phone. Any stand. Steady beats sharp every time, and it frees both your hands. 2 · Light in front of you, not behind. A window at midday is free and better than most lamps. If you film after dark, one light in front is the whole upgrade. 3 · Sound, if people talk. Get the phone closer, or get a small microphone. Nobody leaves because the picture is soft. They leave because they cannot hear you.

Do these in order and stop as soon as it looks fine. There is no fourth thing you need.
