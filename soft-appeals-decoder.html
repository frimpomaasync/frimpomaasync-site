The two letters in front decide who owes.

Before the number means anything, the prefix says whose money it is.

CO

Contractual. The practice absorbs it.

Never billable to the patient. Correct it or appeal it, because nobody else is paying.

PR

Patient responsibility.

Bill the patient. PR-1, PR-2 and PR-3 (deductible, coinsurance, copay) are normal cost sharing, not denials at all.

OA · PI

Other adjustment · payer-initiated.

OA is usually bookkeeping between insurances. PI has no contract behind it, which is exactly why it is often disputable.

How long you have, payer by payer.

Maryland law sets the floor: at least 90 working days from the denial to appeal, for every Maryland-regulated carrier and Medicaid MCO. Many payers give more. Medicare and self-funded employer plans run on their own clocks.
