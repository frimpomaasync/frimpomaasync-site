The twelve areas, and what each one is worth

The weighting is published because a score you cannot check is a score you cannot use.

Each question is worth 0 to 4 points, multiplied by the weight below. Owner dependence and invoicing carry the heaviest weight because both stop money: work that halts when you are away, and work already finished that never gets paid for. Follow-up and re-entered information carry less, because those cost time and margin rather than the job itself.

The arithmetic. Your score is the points you earned divided by the points that were available, as a percentage. A question you skip is left out of both halves, so a partial run is scored against what you actually answered rather than marked down for what you did not reach.

score = earned ÷ available × 100

Gaps are ranked by what each one costs, which is (4 − your points) × weight. That is why a heavy area answered badly always sits above a light one, and why two people with the same score can get a different first move.

Nothing here is a benchmark against other businesses. There is no dataset behind this and it would be dishonest to imply one. It is a structured read of twelve answers you gave, and the value is in the order it puts them in.
