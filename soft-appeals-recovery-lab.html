Why there is no score

Reasons you can check, not a number you have to trust.

Every recommendation in the Lab is explained by the same kind of factors: what the denial states, what the available documentation shows, which pathways were considered, and what is still missing. That is what Show me why opens on every claim.

What you will not find is a percentage next to any claim. A probability score would be a prediction dressed up as data, and it would tell you nothing about what to do next. The factors tell you exactly what to do next, and they can be checked against your own records.
