The quote you sent is sitting in someone's inbox.

You did the visit, worked out the number, wrote it up, and sent it. Then silence. Most of the time silence is not a no. It is a busy person who meant to reply, and a competitor who followed up first.

Follow up on day two, day five, and day nine.

Day two: check the quote arrived and ask if anything needs explaining. Day five: offer to walk through it on a five-minute call. Day nine: ask plainly if the job is still happening, and say the quote holds until a date. Then stop. Three touches wins back the winnable ones without pestering anyone.

Why it works

A held date beats a hovering price.

Naming the date the quote expires gives a busy person a reason to decide this week. It also gives you a clean end: after the date, the quote closes and you stop carrying it in your head.
