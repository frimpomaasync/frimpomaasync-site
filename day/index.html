how this thing worksRead one section. Close it. Come back when you need the next one.

start here, three taps

  1. Tap your energy. The big number on the right. 1 is empty, 5 is full. Be honest.
  2. Tap what broke. Plan intact, lost under 30, lost over 60, wiped out, or home day.
  3. Tap Start. The clock runs. Do the one task in yellow. Tap Done.
That is the whole daily loop. Everything else is optional.

one list on every device

  1. Scroll to the Every device card. Tap Turn it on. This phone now saves to your own site.
  2. Tap Copy the pairing link. Send it to yourself. Open it on the Mac, the other phone, wherever.
  3. That device pulls the same list. From then on every tap lands everywhere within a minute.
The link holds your key. Keep it to yourself. Nothing else identifies you: no login, no email.

calendar on the phone

  1. On a computer open Google Calendar. Tap the gear, then Settings.
  2. On the left tap your calendar. Scroll to Secret address in iCal format. Copy it.
  3. Back in the planner, the calendar card asks for it once. Paste. Save it.
That address opens your whole calendar to anyone who has it. Paste it here and nowhere else. Repeating events count: school alarms, standing calls, holidays.

repeating tasks

Add a task and set Repeats: every day, weekdays, or every week. Tick it Done and it comes back on its own the next time. "Reply to emails" never needs adding twice.

what came out of it

After Done, the five minute card asks one optional line: what came out of it. Sunday the receipt lists them. That list is next week's content seeds.

copy everything, paste it back

Under the lanes, Copy everything copies your whole planner as text. Paste it into the From Som box on any device and tap Restore everything. Safari can wipe a site's storage; this is the way back.

the seven day strip

Under the big day number, seven small days. A red number is how many hard dates land that day. Tap one and the lanes open.

been away

Three days or more with no check-in and the top screen says so, once, and hides the receipt. Tank first, one task. No streak, no guilt.

tips

  • Rate before coffee. The honest number is the one before the caffeine.
  • First 2 minutes is the trick. Fill it for every deep task. "Open the file. Write one bad sentence." Starting is the hard part.
  • Never skip the buffer twice in a row. Skipping once is fine. Twice is how the afternoon disappears.
  • Home day is a mode. Twenty minute chips add up. The receipt will prove it on Sunday.
  • Fill the empty tank lane on Sunday night. Five reading or sorting tasks. Every wiped out day this week has something to eat.
  • Pick tomorrow at 6 PM, not at bedtime. Tired brains pick badly.
  • Say it beats tapping. Hold Right Option, say "energy three lost an hour", release, Enter.
  • Ignore the pattern until 14. Under 14 check-ins the curve is a guess. After, trust it over your gut.
  • Due date on everything with a real date. Undated tasks never jump the gate.

iphone shortcuts, step by step

Two shortcuts. Each one is one tap on the home screen and the day is rerouted before you unlock.

  1. Open the Shortcuts app. Tap + top right.

The rules it runs
  • The lock. Below 3, full tank work locks. Forcing it buys high effort for low quality.
  • Hard dates jump the line. Anything due within 3 days sits on top, gate or no gate. When the tank is low it asks for a 20 minute chip, not the whole block.
  • The swap. A killed deep block becomes empty tank time. Chaos feeds the second goal.
  • Small hit, under 30 minutes. Clear 2 or 3 quick half tank items, then resume.
  • Big hit, over an hour. Scrub the fixed schedule. Flexible work or recovery only.
  • Wiped out, energy 2 or under. Active recovery now. Protect tomorrow's deep session.
  • Your pattern. After 14 check-ins it predicts your energy by hour and names your real deep window.
  • Calendar read. Pulls today's events. Any block over an hour still ahead is flagged as a big hit before you feel it. Kid events suggest a home day. The biggest free gap is matched to the longest deep task that fits.
  • Home day. Kids home, sick day, no school. Twenty-minute blocks only. Long tasks become 20 minute chips. A timer runs each block.
  • One thing. The top of the page shows one task, its first two minutes, and a timer. Nothing else until you ask for the rest.
  • Tomorrow, tonight. After 6 PM it asks for tomorrow's one deep task. In the morning it is already on top, no deciding.
  • Five minutes of nothing. After a block ends, five minutes of nothing before the next task shows. You can skip it. It never skips itself.
  • Body double. One tap opens a fresh video room in a new tab and starts 25 minutes. Work with someone on screen.
  • The vanish check. Ninety minutes past a block with no tap and the top screen goes dark: you vanished, eat something.
  • Sleep and coffee. Both ride along with every check-in. After ten, the forecast says what they do to your peak.
  • Lock screen. Open the link with #home or #say=energy 3 lost an hour and the day is rerouted before you unlock. An iPhone Shortcut does it in one tap.
  • Say it. Type or dictate "energy 3, lost an hour" and it sets the tank, the hit, and logs the check-in.
  • Every device. One key, one list, saved to your own site on every tap. The 4:44 brief reads the same file.
  • Repeats. A repeating task comes back the morning after you tick it.
  • What came out of it. One optional line after Done. The receipt keeps them.
  • Been away. Three days without a check-in and the receipt hides until the first tap of the day.
