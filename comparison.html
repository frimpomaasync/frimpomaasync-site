The four options, stated fairly.

Each one is genuinely the right answer for somebody. The question is which somebody you are.

Option 1

Hire an operations person.

The most capable option, and the most expensive. They handle judgment, exceptions and everything nobody wrote down. They also need managing, they take holidays, and when they leave, what they learned leaves with them.

Option 2

Buy software and configure it yourself.

Cheapest on paper. The bill is your hours, not the licence, and it never appears on a pricing page. It also means you now own a second job: keeping the configuration current as the business changes.

Option 3

Carry on exactly as you are.

A real option, and sometimes the right one. It costs nothing today. What it costs is the hours the Operations Map would put a number on, every month, forever, plus the ceiling it puts on how big you can get.

Option 4

Have it built once, and own it.

Five roles mapped, built, tested, approved and handed over in accounts in your name. One fee. No salary, no subscription to me, and nothing that leaves. This is Siesie.

What none of them fix

A process nobody has written down.

Hire someone into a business that runs on memory and they spend six months learning the memory. Buy software for it and you automate confusion at speed. Every option on this page gets better after the mapping, which is why the map comes first and costs $2,500 rather than $25,000.

What I am not comparing

An agency, or a bigger firm.

If you want a team, a contract with a company behind it, and someone to escalate to at 2am, hire a firm. That is a legitimate thing to want and I am not it. What you get here instead is the person who scoped it being the person who builds it.

When not to choose Siesie. Read this part properly.

Every provider has a version of this page. Most of them forget to write this section.

Do not

If only one handoff is the problem.

Document that one handoff and keep your money. The five-role check on the Siesie page will tell you which it is, free, in about a minute.

Do not

If the work needs judgment all day.

If almost nothing repeats, there is nothing to build around. Hire a person. I will tell you this on the call rather than after the invoice.

Do not

If it is inquiries, not operations.

Calls and messages falling through is the front desk, and that starts at $555. Buying the $25,000 build for a $555 problem is a mistake I will not take your money for.

Do not

If you cannot give access or approvals.

The seven-day build depends on decisions arriving when the work is waiting on them. If nobody can give those in a working week, the timing is wrong and we should wait.
