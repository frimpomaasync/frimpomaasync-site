The third kind: the paperwork you would receive.

Ask for any of these before you commit to anything. A provider who cannot produce them on request has not built them.

01

The scope of work

Line by line, per role, with what is included, what is not, and the acceptance criterion that decides whether each one is finished.

02

Commercial terms

Milestones, change orders priced before the work, ownership, termination, and what happens to your money if I walk away.

03

The responsibility split

One page saying who does what, so neither of us assumes the other is handling something.

04

The risk register

Including the risk you are too polite to raise: what happens if I disappear mid-build. In writing, with the mitigation beside it.

05

The weekly status report

What moved, what is next, what I am waiting on from you, and whether the date changed. Every Friday, including the quiet ones.

06

The data handling page

Where your data lives, what access I hold, what I keep, for how long, and what you can revoke at handover.
