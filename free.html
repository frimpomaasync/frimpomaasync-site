The missed-call response

Use these four messages today.

Replace the brackets with your business, area, and available times. Keep the script in your notes so you can send it without rewriting it.

1 · "Hi, you've reached [your business]. Sorry we missed you. What do you need?" 2 · "Got it. What area are you in, and how soon do you need it?" 3 · "We can do [day] at [time] or [day] at [time]. Which works?" 4 · "Booked. You'll get a reminder the night before. Reply here anytime."

If nobody books after the first text, call within an hour. Send one useful reminder the next morning and offer two real times.
