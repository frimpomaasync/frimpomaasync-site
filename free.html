The 5-minute text-back script · copy it
"Hi, this is [your name] from [your business]. Sorry I missed you, I was on a job. Are you calling about [your main service]? I have [today 4:30] or [tomorrow 9 AM]. Which works better?"
Text it to yourself

Send it within 5 minutes of any missed call. Two time options, one question. That's the whole trick. You answer first and make booking a one-word reply.

The cadence
1Within 5 min: the text above
2Within 1 hour: one call, voicemail is fine, mention the text
3Next morning: "Still need help with [service]? Today I have [two slots]."
The honest catch: this works, as long as you remember, every time, on a roof, at dinner, on a Saturday. SynKasa is just this playbook running automatically, so no lead ever waits on your memory.
