What you keep

Seven documents, and they stay yours whatever happens next.

The Operations Map, the Test Record, your written approvals, the Launch Log, the Operating Manual, the Ownership Pack, and the weekly updates from the whole build. If we never work together again, another provider could pick this up from the paperwork alone.

Why it works this way

A documented system is worth more than a working one.

A working system nobody wrote down is a system that depends on whoever built it. That is fine until you want to change something, hire someone, or stop working with me. Documented, it is an asset your business owns.

The standard every build has to pass, printed in full.

Most providers will tell you they test. Here is the actual list, so you can hold me to it, or hand it to whoever you hire instead.

If a line on this list cannot be ticked, it does not launch. The date moves and you hear it from me the same day.

What stays human

Judgment, exceptions, approvals, and anything sensitive.

The repeating path moves without waiting on you. Decisions that need you still reach you, in the place you already look. That boundary is set by you during onboarding, not assumed by me during the build.

What you bring

The real process, and answers when I ask for them.

Access to your own accounts, your working rules, and a decision when the build is waiting on one. Delay from my side is on me. Delay from yours pauses the clock, and I tell you in writing the day it happens.

Live in 7 days, or you don't pay.

That is a promise about the build, and it is the only promise I make.

What I guarantee

The agreed build goes live on time.

Scoped in writing, tested against the standard above, approved by you, live within seven days of the start date. If I miss it, you do not pay.

What I do not guarantee

A number of bookings.

How many people book you depends on how many were asking in the first place. Anyone promising you a booking number does not know your inquiry volume, and neither do they know your market.

What that means for you

The risk is your time, not your money.

Two calls and some access. If the build is late, it costs you nothing. That is the deal, and it is the same deal for every build.
