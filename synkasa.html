/01

The quiet way money leaves

A missed call is not a missed call. It is a customer deciding you are closed.

You already know this one. The call comes while you are under a sink, on a ladder, mid-appointment, driving. By the time you are free enough to listen to the voicemail, they have booked with somebody who picked up. Nothing broke. Nobody complained. The money just went somewhere else.

The part that stings is that you never see it. A lost customer does not send you a message explaining they went elsewhere. They are simply a call you meant to return, sitting in your phone, going quietly cold.

The one number worth knowing. Harvard Business Review studied thousands of sales leads and found that firms responding within an hour were around seven times more likely to have a meaningful conversation than those waiting even two hours. Under five minutes, the effect was larger still.

Source: The Short Life of Online Sales Leads, Harvard Business Review, 2011. It is the only response-time number I quote, because it is the only one I can point you at.

/08

Questions owners actually ask

Will it sound like a robot?

Go and try it at the top of this page before you decide. It uses your words, your prices and your hours. Most callers ask their question, get the answer, and book. If someone asks for you specifically, it says so and passes them over rather than pretending.

What happens when it does not know something?

It says it does not know and gets you. It is built to hand over rather than guess, because a confident wrong answer about your pricing costs you more than a pause.

Do I need to be technical?

No. You give me your hours, your prices, your calendar and how you like to talk to people. I build it and hand it over. If you can send a voice note, you can do your side of this.

Does this replace my receptionist?

If you have one, no. It takes the after-hours calls, the overflow, and the same eight questions they answer forty times a week, so they can do the work only a person can do. Most owners buying this do not have a receptionist and are not going to hire one.

What is the difference between this and the Client Catcher?

SynKasa answers. The Client Catcher runs the whole loop: catches the lead, follows up in your voice over days, books the job, asks for the review. If your problem is a ringing phone nobody picks up, start here. If it is leads going cold after the first hello, that is the Client Catcher.

Can I move up a tier later?

Yes, and you only pay the difference. Nothing gets rebuilt from scratch.
